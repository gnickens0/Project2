This piece became a lot more abstract than I was initally planning it to be, however I think that works for it pretty well.
It's become something that has reflected me more recently, and how my mind has felt trying to focus on my work and but wanting to be constantly stimulated.
It sucks, I see a therapist about it but it's kind of helped to randomly generate this sort of vision of how it feels trying to work and also get a solution out.
It's why the pattern slips out of the outline of the girl, also because I couldn't get it any tighter within the lines, it's like my thoughts or self control leaving me.
The circles clustering around the head at every iteration is also intentional as it felt like the representation of losing your thoughts so to speak.
I like that it isn't perfect either, it's messy because my mind is messy.
I would say the hardest part was using the join shape function to make the hair, finding that function was life saving but it took a lot of trial and error.
